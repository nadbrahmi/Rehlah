Why Rehlah exists
"I watched my brother navigate cancer for years — alone between every appointment."

Ten years ago, Nadia's brother was diagnosed with lung cancer. She sat with him through every cycle, every nadir window, every appointment. She watched him struggle to communicate what he was feeling to his doctors — not because he lacked words, but because nothing was designed to help him track it. The system never asked between visits.

Since then, Nadia has spent a decade alongside cancer patients — as a caregiver, a companion, and a fierce advocate. She has helped patients through chemotherapy, guided families through the terror of nadir windows, and watched clinicians walk into consultations with almost no information about how their patients had actually been.

Rehlah is the product that she could not find when she needed it. Built not from research, but from ten years of sitting in the gap. Designed for the Arabic-speaking patient — in her language, in her culture, at every hour the healthcare system cannot reach her.

How it works

From your hospital to your pocket — and back

Rehlah is the bridge between the clinical data your hospital holds and the daily life only you live.

1

Hospital seeds your profile

Your oncologist uploads your protocol — drug names, cycle dates, nadir window. Rehlah pre-loads everything before you open the app for the first time.

2

Check in daily — 2 minutes

Each check-in is personalised to your exact cycle day. On Day 10 of AC-T, Rehlah asks about fatigue and fever. On Day 3, it asks about nausea and appetite.

3

Your care team is always informed

If your symptoms cross a threshold, your care coordinator receives an alert before you call in crisis. The early warning reaches the right person, automatically.

4

Pre-visit report, 48 hours before

Your oncologist walks into every appointment already knowing how you have been — in the context of your exact protocol and cycle phase.
