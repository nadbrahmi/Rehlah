Pre-visit report

Walk into every appointment already knowing

The report is structured in four sections — all generated automatically from 14 days of patient check-in data, correlated with their specific protocol and cycle phase.

①
Protocol Context
Cycle number, day, nadir status, check-in completion rate — generated without AI from structured data.
②
Phase-Specific Symptom Flags
Only flags what is unexpected for this exact cycle phase. HIGH / MODERATE / STABLE — defined by your clinical advisor.
③
Medication Adherence
Per-medication adherence rate, missed dose dates, and the reason given by the patient. Refill flag if supply is running low.

AI Talking Points
Three protocol-aware questions for the consultation, bilingual (Arabic / English). Uses the patient's own words where available. The only section requiring the Claude AI model.

Safety & data governance

Clinical AI with hard boundaries

Rehlah's AI is supportive, not diagnostic. Every alert threshold and report structure is co-defined with a clinical advisor. The AI never overrides clinical judgment.

🇦🇪
UAE data residency — Day 1
All patient data is stored within UAE jurisdiction from first deployment. Supabase project configured to Middle East / UAE region. NABIDH HL7 FHIR R4-compatible schema built in from day one.
🔒
Role-based access control
Coordinators see only their own patients. Oncologists see only their assigned patients. Patient data is never accessible across departments without explicit permission.
⚕️
Clinician-defined thresholds
Alert thresholds are defined per symptom, per protocol, per phase by a MASCC Center of Excellence-affiliated clinical advisor — not by algorithms. The AI flags; the clinician decides.
🚫
Patient data never sold
Individual patient data is never sold or shared with third parties under any circumstances. Anonymised population-level data for research is handled under explicit institutional data sharing agreements only.
📋
GDPR-equivalent consent
Explicit bilingual (Arabic / English) consent flow at patient onboarding. Patients know exactly what data is collected, who sees it, and how to delete it. Data minimisation enforced by architecture.
🧪
PRO-CTCAE & EORTC QLQ-C30 backbone
Symptom tracking is built on PRO-CTCAE (NCI validated, 124 items across 78 toxicities, validated in Arabic) and EORTC QLQ-C30 (30-item quality-of-life instrument, ESMO standard). No proprietary symptom scales.
